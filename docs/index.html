RRA first-five launch protocol
1. Select exactly five HVAC targets.
Residential, owner-operated, 2-15 techs. No franchises, agencies, commercial-only shops, or firms with full-time marketing staff.
2. Audit each target.
Run the RRA audit, then manually verify the top revenue leak.
3. Do the 20-minute truth pass.
Reviews, mobile call/booking path, after-hours handling, service promise. Upgrade assumptions to KNOWN wherever possible.
4. Generate five client-facing proposals.
Lead with one evidence-backed number and the $997 Good wedge. Never send the internal view.
5. Send and follow up.
Day 0 first touch, Day 4 follow-up, Day 11 final launch follow-up. Every sent prospect gets a next-action date.
6. Read the market.
2-3 sales: deliver and measure. Zero sales with conversations: change one variable. Zero replies: change target/message/evidence, not product architecture.
Launch freeze

No dashboard, CRM integration, second vertical, billing system, autonomous execution, orchestrator expansion, architecture refactor, or paid lead stack before the first-five cycle produces evidence.
