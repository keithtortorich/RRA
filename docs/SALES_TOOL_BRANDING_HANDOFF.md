# NETBUILD.PRO Sales Tool Branding Handoff

> **SUPERSEDED — 2026-09-19.** Reversed by founder decision. Current brand is **RRA — Revenue Recovery Agency / RRA Diagnostic Terminal**. Historical record only; `docs/index.html` is current.


**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** September 19, 2026  
**Applies to:** `docs/index.html` and the `SALES TOOL.html` symlink  
**Status:** Implementation contract

This document translates the NETBUILD.PRO brand system into rules for the live Sales Tool. If the Sales Tool conflicts with this handoff or the primary brand contract, the primary brand contract wins.

## 1. Product role

The Sales Tool is **Sizzle**, the internal operator surface used by NETBUILD.PRO to move a prospect through:

**Verify → Diagnose → Share → Follow up → Close → Onboard**

It is not a public SaaS product, a client portal, or a third-party agency tool. Do not market, price, or describe Sizzle as licensable software.

Stella is the separate client-facing delivery surface. Stella does not appear as a selectable Sales Tool product or independent subscription.

## 2. Visible identity

| Element | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Brand | **NETBUILD.PRO** |
| Descriptor | **REVENUE RECOVERY FIRM** |
| Tool label | **SIZZLE · SALES TOOL** |
| Tool line | **Find the leak. Recover the revenue.** |
| Primary tagline, when used | **Stop losing revenue already entering your business.** |

RRA may remain in internal code, storage keys, schemas, IDs, comments, and repository paths. It must not appear as the visible company name, wordmark, product label, or customer-facing acronym.

## 3. Wordmark and Seal

- Use the approved Seal without changing its geometry.
- Set **NETBUILD** in Newsreader Medium.
- Set **.PRO** smaller, in Brass, on the same baseline.
- Wordmark tracking is `0.08em`.
- The `.PRO` dot is part of the suffix and is also Brass.
- Use the Seal alone for compact app-icon treatment.
- Do not place a letter or monogram inside the Seal.

## 4. Interface direction

The Sales Tool is a dark forensic workstation, not a generic SaaS dashboard.

- Main field: Ink `#12110F`
- Elevated surfaces: Ink 800 `#1C1B18`
- Borders: Ink 700 `#2A2824` or restrained light rules
- Readouts and evidence documents: Bone `#F4F1EA` or Paper `#FAF7F1`
- Corners: sharp or no more than 2px radius
- Visual density: operational and evidence-led
- Primary hierarchy: evidence, next action, validated value
- Avoid gradients, glass effects, neon, chatbot chrome, oversized pills, and decorative dashboard tiles

## 5. Color semantics

| Color | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Brass `#C5A572` | Brand accent, Seal keystone, primary action, active navigation, selected state |
| Brass Deep `#6E5428` | Accessible Brass text on Bone |
| Recovered `#2F5A40` | Confirmed and measured recovered revenue only |
| Recovered Soft `#E3EDE6` | Confirmed recovery badges and restrained washes |
| Leak `#8F2E28` | Active leakage, failure, or warning |
| Leak Soft `#F3E4E2` | Leak and warning badges |
| Stone `#6F6A62` | Secondary text and metadata |

Hard rules:

- Estimated opportunity is not recovered revenue.
- Unverified dollar figures must not use Recovered green.
- Brass does not mean recovered revenue.
- Recovered and Leak are functional data colors and never belong in the wordmark or Seal.

## 6. Typography and numbers

- Display and wordmark: Newsreader Medium
- Body and interface: Source Sans 3
- Audit IDs and scan IDs: Source Code Pro
- Use tabular lining figures for money, percentages, dates, and counts.
- Use sentence case for headings.
- Reserve uppercase for the wordmark, descriptor, compact labels, and status markers.
- Numbers are the visual hero only when their evidence state is visible.

## 7. Evidence-state rules

Every finding and dollar amount must expose its status.

| State | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Unverified | Clearly labeled; neutral styling; cannot support the $997 offer |
| Estimated | Show the conservative basis and source; neutral or Brass treatment |
| Validated | Eligible for diagnosis and prioritization; still not Recovered green |
| Confirmed recovered | May use Recovered green after baseline and outcome measurement |

Every finding must show:

1. The leak
2. Evidence source
3. Evidence date
4. Conservative value or value range
5. Confidence or validation state
6. Specific next action

## 8. Stage requirements

### Verify

- Keep the five-item verification checklist visible.
- Identify the specific unfinished item, not a generic incomplete status.
- Block client-facing dollar claims until the supporting evidence is validated.
- Use an amber warning state for incomplete evidence and a restrained green state only for ready-to-share status.

### Diagnose

- Rank validated opportunities by conservative recoverable value, confidence, effort, and time to impact.
- Good must always match the single highest-priority validated leak.
- Do not allow manual offer selection to override the guardrail.

### Share

- The Client Packet must be a one-page evidence document.
- Each finding follows: **Leak → conservative value → recovery action**.
- Include source and date for every finding.
- Include copy, print, and sent-tracking actions.
- Exclude internal scores, agent names, worker names, and unsupported calculations.

### Follow up

- Show the next action on every active prospect card.
- Preserve the evidence state and agreed offer through follow-up.
- Do not convert an estimate into a promise in follow-up copy.

### Close

- Use the approved offer schedule only:
  - Free: evidence scan
  - Good: $997 fixed-fee implementation for the single highest-priority validated leak
  - Better: $5,000 setup plus $2,500 per month for the top three validated opportunities
  - Best: $7,500 per month for full ongoing revenue-recovery management
- No paid-audit price range, revenue share, performance fee, separate Stella subscription, or Sizzle licensing.

### Onboard

- Open the handoff checklist when a prospect moves to WON.
- Show the agreed fix and first implementation step.
- Require a baseline-measurement field.
- Require the minimum-access checklist before implementation begins.
- Continue showing the specific unfinished onboarding item on the board.

## 9. Bench and board behavior

- Bench is the unlimited raw-contact list that feeds the five active launch slots.
- The active board must remain capped at five launch prospects unless the operating doctrine changes.
- Every card needs one visible next action.
- Dossier paste-and-parse may populate fields, but imported claims remain unverified until checked.
- Status color must communicate workflow state, not decorate the interface.

## 10. Copy rules

Use language that is short, commercially direct, financially literate, and evidence-driven.

Preferred patterns:

- “Evidence found”
- “Needs verification”
- “Validated leak”
- “Conservative opportunity”
- “Ready to share”
- “Measure baseline”
- “Recover this first”

Forbidden language:

- AI-powered
- 10X
- growth engine
- revolutionary
- game-changing
- supercharge
- unlock your potential
- guaranteed revenue
- revenue already earned

Do not drift into “we run your marketing.” NETBUILD.PRO finds, validates, and recovers trapped revenue.

## 11. Export and print rules

- Client-facing documents use NETBUILD.PRO, never RRA.
- Use US Letter with 1-inch margins.
- Use an Ink cover or header and Bone/Paper interior.
- Preserve source, date, evidence state, and conservative basis in printed output.
- Do not print internal navigation, worker names, internal scoring detail, or debug state.
- The browser tab, printable packet, copied text, and social metadata must all use the same public identity.
